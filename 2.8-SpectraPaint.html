How to use. Drag on the spectrum to erase frequencies (they turn dark) and watch the result update instantly. Bright isolated dots away from the centre are periodic patterns (stripes, fences) — erase them, or press Detect & remove peaks. Switch the result to removed part only to see exactly what you deleted. Turn off Mirror brush to see why a real image's spectrum must be symmetric: erasing only one side creates an imaginary part (leakage) and a weaker, incomplete removal.

Every spectrum value has a magnitude (how strong each stripe pattern is) and a phase (where the stripes are positioned). Here the two are swapped between images.

The result looks like the image whose phase was used: phase carries the structure (edges, shapes), while magnitude mostly carries overall texture and contrast.
